Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This explains what our social media tools do with your information. We have kept it short and in plain English.

Who we are

Kybur Pty Ltd, ACN 698 865 235, on the Gold Coast in Queensland, Australia. You can reach us at blvckosapp@gmail.com.

What these tools are

We run a small set of private tools that connect to the Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages that Kybur owns or manages. They connect through Meta's official developer platform. Only our own team can use them. Nobody outside Kybur can sign up or log in.

What we collect

  • When you comment on one of our posts: your username, your comment and the time you posted it.
  • When you message one of our accounts: your username, your profile name and picture, the messages in that conversation and the time of each one.
  • About our own accounts: our posts and their statistics, such as views, likes and shares. These are totals and do not name you.

We do not collect your password, your contacts, your location or anything from your account that you have not sent to us or posted on our pages.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, to keep track of conversations so nobody is missed or asked the same thing twice, and to see which of our posts are working.

Who sees it

Only the Kybur team. We do not sell your information and we do not give it to anyone else for marketing. A few services help us run things on our behalf, such as hosting and databases, and they handle your information only for that purpose.

Where it is kept

In private databases that need a login. Some of the services we use store information outside Australia.

How long we keep it

For as long as the conversation is useful to us, and no longer than 24 months after your last message or comment. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. The steps are on the data deletion page. You can also delete your own comments and unsend your own messages on Instagram and Facebook, and stop messaging us whenever you like.

Complaints

Tell us first and we will try to fix it. If you are still not happy, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change this page we will update the date at the top.
