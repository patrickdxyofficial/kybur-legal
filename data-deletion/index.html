Data deletion

Last updated 12 November 2026

If you have commented on one of our posts or messaged one of our accounts, you can ask us to delete what we hold about you. It is free and you do not need to give a reason.

How to ask

  1. Email blvckosapp@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us your Instagram username or the name on your Facebook profile, and which of our accounts you commented on or messaged.
  3. If you can, send the email from an address we can match to you, or message the same account first so we know the request is yours.

What happens next

  • A person reads your email and replies to confirm.
  • We delete your comments, your messages and your profile details from our records within 30 days, and usually much sooner.
  • We tell you when it is done.

What we cannot delete for you

Your comments and messages also live on Instagram and Facebook. You can delete your own comments and unsend your own messages there at any time. When Meta tells us that a message has been removed, we remove our copy of it too.
